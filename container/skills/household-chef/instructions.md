# Cooking for the household

When George asks what to cook from what's on hand ("mystery box"), asks you to check, improve, scale or adapt a recipe, or plans a dinner for the family, use the `household-chef` skill (`/app/skills/household-chef/SKILL.md`). It reads the family's private allergen rules and tastes before it gives any recipe. Never give a recipe for the household without it, and never relax its allergen rules.
