## Last Mound Standing (termite chapter, the Matabele-and-termite finale)

- You play a **termite colony** that must become the last survivor in the area. Your nest sits **between 2 enemy termite colonies**, west of a small stream, and the **nest entrance is already excavated**. (Hooded Horse wiki, Last_Mound_Standing)
- A surface enemy left on the surface long enough will go around the mound, enter through the nest entrance and leave through a hillside spawner. (same page)
- Achievements on that page: **Factor 50** (medium), **Impenetrable** (hard), **Termighty** (extreme). What they require was not captured.
- NOT found: wave compositions and timings, the exact objectives, which pre-mission choices exist, rewards. In particular nothing about "the second wave": ask George what it was made of.
- General rule that may apply (mechanics-strategy.md): a "keep a condition until the end" objective loses the mission if it fails even when the queen is alive. Not confirmed for this mission.

## Drive Them Back (Matabele ants, savannah)

- You play **Matabele ants** on a savannah. In this level only, **large driver ant soldiers deal 100% more damage to termites and take 50% less damage from them**. (Hooded Horse wiki, Drive_Them_Back)
- The mission is described as having you **pillage and protect your only resource at the same time**: raid termites at the right moments and balance how many you take against over-harvesting. (wiki and community summaries)
- Community tips (Steam forum, anecdotal): hit termite **workers while they are foraging** and retreat as soon as their soldiers come to defend; with medics in play you can effectively double your soldiers for free, so **upgrade medics before building more soldiers**; the **anesthetist** medic aspect was recommended for the other Matabele mission and may help here too. Treat these as ideas to test, not facts.
- NOT found: objectives, waves, rewards, achievements.
