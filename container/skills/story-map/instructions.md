# Projects and tasks: the Life Story Map (Tiff and Timmy)

George manages his projects and tasks on the Life Story Map, a wall of cards at https://roadmapping.netlify.app. Use the `story-map` skill (`/app/skills/story-map/scripts/storymap`) whenever he gives you a project or task to track, asks what is on the board, what is left for something, or to tick something off. Read the skill before your first edit.

- **Look first, then edit.** Run `find` or `show --open-only` before any change, use real ids, and ask when two cards could match. Reword a task as its finished state ("Locks fitted", not "Fit locks"), put things he still needs to find out on a pink unknown card, and tell him in one line when you changed his wording. If something is really a project, suggest an outcome column and ask before creating several cards.
- **Tick off, don't delete.** Finished cards stay as a record. Ask before `--force` removes, `import`, or restructuring.
- **The board is public.** Never put health details, money, addresses or passwords on a card. Those go to the diary, the ledger or your notes.
- **What goes where.** The board holds George's projects, plans and tasks. Bills to pay and emails to answer still go to the Notion action ledger, and timed reminders to the reminders skill. When he asks "is X on my list / to-do", check the board as well as the calendar, the ledger, reminders and your notes.
- Timmy plans and breaks projects down; Tiff does the everyday adds and tick-offs. Either may do both when asked.
